Bird sound detector

Recognises 182 bird species of the Western Ghats. The model runs on this device and no audio is uploaded.

About and tips

Each 5-second window is converted to a mel spectrogram and scored by a small distilled MobileNetV3 network (about 1.7 M parameters). Detections need a probability of at least 50%.

Recording tips: stay still, get as close to the bird as you safely can, and avoid wind and speech. Phone voice processing is switched off so birdsong is not filtered out.

This is a research demo. The model was trained on clean recordings, so noisy field audio can produce false detections or misses. Treat results as hints, not identifications.
